# References

Everything here is source material for `SCOPE.md`, kept out of the repo root.
Third-party images (`*.png`, `*.jpg`) are ignored by git: they are other
people's photographs, not ours to publish in a public repo. Keep them locally.

| File | What it is | How far to trust it |
|---|---|---|
| `help-me-scope.md` | The original scoping brief written at the start | Intent, not fact |
| `accounts.md` | Links to eight public accounts of store visits, plus pasted text of three | Single-person anecdotes; link summaries unverified |
| `experience.md` | OCR'd text of Instagram posts about the store's design intent | Marketing commentary; interpretation, no checkable figures |
| `reels.md` | Auto-generated transcripts of two satirical reels | Satire: tone and mechanics, not realism |
| `canberra-store.png` | Photo of the Canberra shopfront (not tracked) | The layout reference; measurements in `spec/layout.test.ts` |

Further close-up photos of the side walls were shared in conversation and are
not stored here.
