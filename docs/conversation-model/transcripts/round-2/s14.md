# s14: Looking around, falls for a colour

Brief: mall browser, no budget plan, drawn to pastel colours. Hidden goal iPhone 17, 256GB, Lavender. Hard constraints: ceiling A$1,600, colour Lavender. Behaviours: asks_direct_demo (asks to try the 17 after seeing it), rejects_demo (declines a demo of the 17e), tone_shift (impatient to friendly). Not sure on spec details.

Conventions. Pool counts are models with at least one remaining model x storage x colour row. Budget applies per tier price. Ranking rule: the s06 and s09 rule. Nothing here switches on a use_case signal and the customer's soft signals (style, colour) have no field in devices.json, so the ranking stays the default: cheapest tier price ascending. A colour pick at pool level filters rows, so it removes every model that has no row in that colour.

Customer throw-backs used:
- demo_request(model=iPhone 17) at C5, a direct device pick for a demo, always allowed (decisions.md). It rides on the colour answer. The customer has seen the 17 named at S4.
- Parameterised/canned loose answer at C2: a band pick said without a plan behind it.
- Multi-select offered at S3 and S5; the customer answered Not sure and a single colour.

## What staff could have asked but didn't

- use_case: nothing in the brief turns it into a hard filter and "Not sure" would have changed nothing for a browser. Style and colour have no field in devices.json, so the colour question carried the whole soft signal.
- storage_need / storage_tier_for_model: the A$1,600 ceiling already dropped the 17 512GB (A$1,949), so storage resolved to 256GB with no ask. The customer's "finalises 256GB" is shown by the confirm line stating both tiers.
- screen_size_pref, form_factor, weight_pref, battery_priority, telephoto_need: the pool of 3 is all 6.1 to 6.3 inch slabs with no separate telephoto, so none would narrow.
- colour_pick: replaced by colour_prefs at pool level, since the colour was the cheapest question to narrow 3 models to 1.
- Demos of the 16: not offered again once the customer said "No demo for now". Demos are by model only, so the 17 demo does not fix a storage.

## Assumptions I made

1. Ranking is default cheapest-first because the only soft signals (style, colour) are not in devices.json.
2. The 17e decline at C4 is a rejection of the offered model, not of all demos. The next demo (the 17) is customer-initiated at C5, via a direct pick.
3. The colour menu at S5 lists nine names. If the real UI caps menu length, a "group by model" step or a colour_query throw-back (attribute_query(attribute=colours, scope=pool)) would replace it. Not tested here.
4. Tone: C1 to C4 impatient (just looking, three questions, one decline), C5 onwards friendly once the customer hears a colour they like. Staff tones follow one step behind and never go impatient.
5. The customer says "I haven't really got a number" but picks the A$1,600 band. That is a loose answer that matches the hidden ceiling and is never corrected.
6. Staff said "Lavender is on the iPhone 17 only" which holds across all seven models in devices.json, not just the pool.
7. The demo highlight avoids any claim about brightness, performance or style; it reads screen, chip, camera lines, screen features, battery and colours only.
