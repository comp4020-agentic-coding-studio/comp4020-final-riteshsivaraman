## Question types used

| qtype | text | options | multi? | PRECONDITION | EFFECT | NEEDS DATA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| known_model | Did you already have a phone in mind? | One option per model in devices.json / A model that's not on this list / Not yet, show me options / Not sure | no | Opening of a phone scene; nothing known | A model pick sets model=X and shrinks pool to that model (direct pick; the same pick can be made at any time). "Not yet" routes to discovery questions | none |
| storage_tier_for_model | The model comes in tiers A at $a, B at $b ... Which one? | The model's tiers from devices.json (all four for 18 Pro) / Not sure | no | Model fixed; more than one tier possible (budget unknown or not binding) | Sets storage and price; budget check is against the tier price | none |
| colour_pick | Which colour do you want? | The model's colours from devices.json / Not sure | no | Model fixed (colours are per model) | Sets colour; no pool change at model level | Per-colour stock (staff can only say "I'll check") |
| confirm_final_choice | Recap model, storage, colour, price. Is that what you want? | Yes, that one / Change the colour / Change the storage / Change the model / Leave without a phone | no | Model, storage and colour all set | Yes ends the scene; any "Change ..." reopens that one attribute | Stock check result |

Move without a qtype: answer_throwback is chained into the next question's line (S5 answers the price ask, then asks colour_pick). The canned price reply has no effect on state (so it cannot loop).

## Customer throw-backs used

- price_ask (C4): "Can the price come down at all?" Canned reply: listed price only, staff have no pricing authority. Effect: none.

## What staff could have asked but didn't

- budget_ceiling: redundant, the customer already named the model and then picked a tier price.
- offer_demo: pool is 1 model and the customer is decided and impatient. A demo does not narrow anything.
- use_case, form_factor, screen_size_pref, camera_setup: nothing to narrow with a pool of 1.
- storage first, then colour: could have been combined into one turn, but each is a separate fixed-option question.
- Staff could have filtered the tier list by budget, but the budget is unknown at S3, so all four tiers are shown.
- A reopen: none needed.

## Assumptions I made

- A direct model pick at known_model collapses the pool to that model; later questions are only the remaining sub-choices (storage, colour).
- price_haggle in this scene gets a canned reply with no effect; staff do not state a discount policy (not in devices.json).
- Staff say "I'll go and check we have it in stock" and make no stock statement.
- Customer tone goes impatient then neutral once the end is in sight (within the scenario's typical tones).
- Hidden budget (A$2,500) is never asked or stated; it only constrains the pick (512GB at A$2,499 fits).
