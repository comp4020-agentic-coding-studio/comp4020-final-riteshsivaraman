Behaviours: picks_unavailable_colour (gold), changes_mind_late (after choosing 18 Pro 1TB, raises the ceiling by A$200 and swaps to the Max for battery). Tone: neutral, then unsure.

## What staff could have asked but didn't

- use_case, telephoto_need, camera_priority: both models in the pool have the separate telephoto, ProRes and 4K 120 fps, so neither question could narrow or change the ranking. The customer's video and telephoto signals matter only to the Pro vs Max call, which the compare answer covered.
- battery_priority: would have surfaced the Max earlier, but the ceiling excluded it, so asking it first would have been a question with no matching option inside the budget. After the raise it was moot.
- size_fit_check: 6.3 vs 6.9 inch would have been a good ask at S12, since the customer is going up to a larger phone. I left it out because the compare answer already gave both sizes and the customer chose.
- Offering the Air demo: it was in the relax menu, the customer declined it. Demos of the Max were offered via "See the iPhone 18 Pro Max first" and not taken.
- storage_tier_for_model: not needed, the 1TB floor and ceiling resolved the tier to 1TB.

## Assumptions I made

1. The ranking is the default cheapest-first because no signal reaches the ranking: video and telephoto are equal across the two models. The Max would lead only once battery counts.
2. "Another colour" lists the store's other colour names. The customer picks Light Gold as their reading of "gold"; there is no plain "Gold" in devices.json, so staff say "gold" only as the family word and name Light Gold exactly.
3. The Air option in the relax menu drops the 18 line but keeps 1TB and the A$3,300 ceiling. The customer declines it; it still follows the "only moves that make the pool non-empty" rule.
4. The reopened model at C10 keeps the Glacier colour. Both Pro models have Glacier, so no re-ask is needed (colour is model-specific and checked).
5. Staff's S11 answers the compare throw-back and asks the budget reopen in one line. The customer's stated reason ("battery gap") is flavour derived from the S11 answer, not read by the system.
6. Tone: neutral through C5, unsure at C6, C7 and C10 (colour unavailable, then a late change), back to neutral and friendly once the choice settles. Staff never shift to impatient.
7. The raise is exactly one band (A$3,300 to A$3,500, A$200 as the brief says) and uses the generated "Raise it to <next breakpoint>" option, not the custom ceiling.
