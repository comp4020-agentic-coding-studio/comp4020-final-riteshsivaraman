Gaps: whether Air or Max suits. Behaviours: throwback_compare, reopen_answer (budget A$3,000 to A$2,000), tone_shift (neutral to unsure after learning the model is gone). Tones: neutral, unsure.

Ranking key (staff only): sum of ranks over the active soft signals (larger screen first; lighter weight first), tie broken by cheaper starting price. Screen size uses the largest screen listed (Duo = 7.6-inch inner). Pool starts at 7 models.

## Question types used

| qtype | text | options | multi? | PRECONDITION | EFFECT | NEEDS DATA |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| known_model | Did you already have a phone in mind? | One option per stocked model / A model that's not on this list / Not yet, show me options / Not sure | no | Opening | "Not on this list" accepts a model_not_stocked throw-back parameterised by a name; sets wanted_model (unstocked) with no pool change | List of unstocked model names the customer can name (not in devices.json; store-side canned list) |
| screen_size_pref | What screen size are you after? | Size bands built from the pool: Compact (about 6.1) / Medium (6.3) / Large (6.5 or bigger) / Not sure | no | Screen size not yet answered; bands must split the pool | Hard filter on largest listed screen; Large leaves Duo, Air, 18 Pro Max; sets "big screen" soft signal | Screen size stated for all models |
| form_factor | Standard phone, or open to a foldable? | Standard phone / Open to a foldable / Not sure | no | Pool contains a foldable and a non-foldable | Standard removes the Duo (only foldable) | Foldable flag is derived from the Duo's open and closed dimensions |
| budget_ceiling | What's the most you'd like to spend? (also used as reopen) | Bands that split the pool: Up to A$2,000 / Up to A$3,000 / No real limit / Not sure | no | Pool has more than one price point | Eliminates models whose cheapest tier is over the ceiling; applies per tier | none |
| weight_pref (NEW) | Does a light, slim phone matter to you? | Yes, as light as possible / A bit / Doesn't matter / Not sure | no | Pool weights differ and weight not yet asked | Soft signal only: ranks by weight ascending. No elimination | Weight stated for all models |
| offer_demo | Want to try either one? | Demo <model> for each remaining model / No demo, keep going | no | 4 or fewer models in pool | None on facts; triggers demo_highlight | Demo colour and stock |
| demo_reaction (post-demo menu) | Happy with this one / Show me the other one / Too expensive for me / Change an earlier answer / Not for me / Not sure yet | as listed | no | A demo just happened | Routes to remaining sub-choices, another demo, reopen, or leave. "Not sure yet" added in this transcript | none |
| colour_pick | Which colour do you want? | The model's colours / Not sure | no | Model fixed | Sets colour | Per-colour stock |
| confirm_final_choice | Recap and confirm | Yes, that one / Change ... / Leave without a phone | no | Model, storage, colour set | Ends or reopens | Stock check |

Skipped by rule: storage_tier_for_model was not asked, because after the reopen only one tier (256GB) fits the ceiling; staff state it inside the colour_pick line.

## Customer throw-backs used

- model_not_stocked(iPhone 16 Plus) (C2): canned "we don't carry that", no claim about the phone itself.
- compare(iPhone Air, iPhone 18 Pro Max) (C10): answered with listed screen, weight, thickness, rear cameras, starting price.

## What staff could have asked but didn't

- one_handed / width: the hidden width of at least 74mm is satisfied by both Air (74.7) and Max (78.0), so asking would not narrow.
- camera_setup / telephoto_need: asked late would have split Air from Max, but the customer gave no camera signal.
- battery_priority: the Max reaches up to 43 hours of video playback and the Air up to 27, which would have split them, but no battery signal was given.
- storage_need: left out because the budget tier decided it.
- relax_requirement: not needed, pool never empty.
- Staff could have reopened the budget earlier (right after the weight pick) instead of after the demo.

## Assumptions I made

- The customer's first budget answer of A$3,000 was a loose guess ("didn't think about it"); the true ceiling A$2,000 is revealed on the staff reopen. This follows the s10 pattern (first answer was Not sure or loose, held-back truth), and it does not contradict the hidden ceiling because A$3,000 is above it and he withdraws it, but the NPC spec must allow it explicitly.
- Staff say only "we don't carry the iPhone 16 Plus" and nothing about its attributes.
- Screen size filter uses the largest listed screen (Duo inner 7.6-inch), so Duo is in the "Large" band until form_factor removes it.
- Demo reaction menu gained "Not sure yet" and "Show me the other one"; not used here.
- Budget bands A$2,000 and A$3,000 are used because they split the pool at the tier level (A$2,000: Air 256GB only; A$3,000: Air and Max up to 512GB).
- Ranking: rank-sum of big screen and light weight, tie broken by cheaper starting price; after weight_pref the sums tie, so the Air leads on price.
- C10 throw-back ("better for me?") gets a factual side-by-side, with no verdict, to respect the no-invention rule.
