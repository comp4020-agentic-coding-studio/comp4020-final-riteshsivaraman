# s04: Family: a 17, but budget too tight

Brief: nurse, wants "a 17 or the cheaper one", ceiling A$1,100, min storage 256GB, colour does not matter. Hidden goal iPhone 17e, 256GB, White after relaxing to A$1,200.

Conventions in this file. Pool counts are models that still have at least one model x storage x colour row. Default ranking with no soft signals is cheapest tier price ascending. Budget applies per tier price. A staff turn of the form `move=answer_throwback | qtype=X` means the staff line answers the throw-back and then asks X in the same turn (assumption 1).

Customer throw-backs used:
- attribute_query(model=iPhone 17e, attribute=Camera Control) at C3. Answered from the 17e buy-page card ("No Camera Control") and the 17 spec page.
- Parameterised pick "Set my own ceiling: A$1,100" at C4.

## What staff could have asked but didn't

- storage_need: skipped because every model in the 17 line starts at 256GB, so a minimum of 256GB or less narrows nothing. The ceiling decided the tier instead (512GB was out at A$1,200), so storage_tier_for_model was never needed either.
- offer_demo at C2 (pool was 2): skipped to narrow on budget first. A staff could offer it there.
- use_case, camera_setup, screen_size_pref (6.1" vs 6.3"): skipped. The pool was 2 and the budget would decide it.
- reopen budget_ceiling to ask "A$1,100 firm or flexible?" before nudging. Not needed, the nudge does the same job.

## Assumptions I made

1. A staff turn can answer a throw-back and ask the next question in one line. Round 1 kept them separate. If the engine does not allow it, split S4 into an answer turn plus a budget turn.
2. Design finding: bands generated only from the pool's breakpoints cannot express a ceiling below the cheapest price, so a zero pool would be unreachable through budget. I added a parameterised "Set my own ceiling (A$100 steps)" pick. The customer used it for A$1,100.
3. Camera Control, Dynamic Island and 120Hz are treated as soft signals, as stated in the brief (Not sure).
4. "17 line" is derived from model names (17 and 17e), as scenarios.md already flagged.
5. The 17e "no" facts (Camera Control, Dynamic Island, ProMotion) come from the buy-page "No ..." cards in devices.json, not the spec page.
6. The nudge says "nothing else we carry is cheaper". Checked across all rows in the script.
7. The default ranking with no signals is cheapest first. This is my rule; the engine may choose otherwise.
8. Staff said the 512GB is over the limit and the 256GB is "the" tier, rather than asking storage. The price list is in devices.json, so no stock claim was made.
