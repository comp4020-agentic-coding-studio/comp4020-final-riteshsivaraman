## What staff could have asked but didn't

- hardware_features, screen_size_pref, form_factor: pool was already 2 after the telephoto answer and the two Pro models differ mainly in size (6.3" vs 6.9"), battery and price. A size_fit_check ("6.9" is big, ok?") would have been a good ask before the demo.
- battery_priority as an explicit question: the Travel pick already put battery into the ranking.
- Asking budget before storage: I asked storage first because it set the price tier.
- Offering the Pro demo as well: the customer chose the Max, and the ranking put it first.
- Colour first (customer has a clear wish for Burgundy) was possible but colour is model-specific and the colour menu only exists once the model is fixed.

## Assumptions I made

1. The ranking rule above is mine. It gives Max 7, Pro 6, Duo 5, and puts the Max first once battery counts, as the brief says. Its weights are arbitrary.
2. Staff said "the longest of anything we carry that lists a range" because the Air and 17e have no optical range line (null in effect). The claim holds for every model that has one.
3. The demo highlight read only fields in devices.json (screen, chip, camera lines, video formats, battery hours, price, colours).
4. Tone shift: C1 to C3 friendly or neutral, impatient at C4 and C5 after four questions, back to neutral and friendly after the pool is 2 and a demo is on offer. Staff S5 acknowledges it ("Last couple, promise") without breaking the tone set.
5. The 2TB rows were dropped by budget, not asked about; the customer asked for "1TB at least" but never said no 2TB, so the ceiling is what resolves it.
6. The customer's "Not sure until told" colour gap is shown by the colour menu being the first time Burgundy is mentioned.
7. Same budget design as s04: a parameterised "Set my own ceiling" pick, because the generated bands (3300, 3500, 4500) do not include A$3,600.
