## Customer throw-backs used

- compare(iPhone 18 Pro, iPhone 18 Pro Max) on battery (C6): answered with both stated figures, no verdict word like "much worse".
- price_ask (C16): "512 for the 256 price?" Canned: listed prices only, no effect.

## What staff could have asked but didn't

- form_factor (foldable?): the battery band already left the Duo in; budget removed it, so a form_factor question never earned its place.
- screen_size_pref and one_handed: size was raised as a size_fit_check after the demo instead. It could have been asked before the budget to avoid showing a model that might be too big.
- camera_setup / telephoto_need: the use_case included photos, but only a ranking effect, and the pool was already 1.
- storage_need ("how full is her current phone?"): the husband's gap is her storage use; he would answer Not sure, so it was left out. It would be a ranking question for tiers only.
- colour_pick before the demo: colour is a hard must (Burgundy) but only becomes relevant after the model is fixed.
- Whether the budget is firm: not needed, he picked a band.

## Assumptions I made

- Video playback hours: first "Up to N" in the battery field. The Duo's 44 hours is the outer display only (31 on the inner), so staff only quote it generically as "reach 40 hours or more", and the pool filter counts it at 44. The Air's 40 hours with MagSafe Battery is an accessory figure and is not used.
- Husband sets hidden threshold 40 hours by picking the "40 or more" band; he never states "40" himself in a way that contradicts his constraint.
- "Biggest standard phone" means the largest screen and widest among non-foldable models (verified by script).
- At pool 1, staff still offer a demo because he wants to check the phone and the demo is by model only. A decided husband might decline; here he accepts.
- Hand-size Not sure: size_fit_check with Not sure applies no filter; staff proceed. If he had said "Too big", the pool would reopen to the 18 Pro (34 hours) and the battery constraint would conflict, requiring a relax_requirement (not exercised).
- Budget band A$2,700 stands for the A$2,699 tier (rounded up).
- The compare answer quotes two numbers from the battery_video_playback field and avoids a verdict.
