What the references say

From the corners you set in the rectify tool (docs/blueprint/rectify.json): the side walls are about 3.35 times as long as they are high, the back wall about 3.3 times as wide as high, and you gave the wall height as 3 m. That makes the shop about 10 m wide by 10 m deep by 3 m high: close to square, and much smaller than the 16 x 15 x 3.8 m used before.

Where the scale is uncertain

  • The 3 m wall height is the one absolute number. The photographs fix the proportions; everything in metres follows from it. If the real ceiling is higher, the whole shop scales with it.
  • Table length, the entrance opening and the back room are assumptions.
  • Wall features (lightboxes, bays, monitors) are placed by their proportion of the wall in the close-up photographs.

Tables: position and orientation
