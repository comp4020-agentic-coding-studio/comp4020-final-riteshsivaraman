# Log a cut

Append to CUTS.md in its existing style: `## YYYY-MM-DD: <what> cut`, then what it was, why (budget, time, a conflict with SCOPE, failed twice at the gate), what remains, and the issue number. Close or relabel the issue with a link to the entry. Append-only.
