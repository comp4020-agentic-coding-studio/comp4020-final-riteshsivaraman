Rectify a wall

The photo: drag the four corners of each wall

Straightened: click two points to measure

    Corners go top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left. Walls of one photo share one camera, so they share one focal length; if two walls that should be the same give different ratios, set one by hand or use "match". Enter one real size you trust (for instance floor to ceiling is 3.8 m) and the other follows from the ratio. Estimates, not a survey: crops and wide-angle lenses throw the camera assumptions off.
