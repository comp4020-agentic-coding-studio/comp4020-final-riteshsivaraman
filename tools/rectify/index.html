Rectify a wall

The photo: drag the four corners of each wall

Straightened: click two points to measure

    Corners go top-left, top-right, bottom-right, bottom-left. Enter one real size you trust (for instance floor to ceiling is 3.8 m); the other follows from the perspective. This assumes square pixels and a camera centred on the photo, so treat the result as an estimate, not a survey. Crops and wide-angle lenses will throw it off.
