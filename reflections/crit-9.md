# Crit 9 reflection

## What was the breakthrough that moved the work forward?

The breakthrough was realising that I did not have to test the 3D scene
itself. A scene is something I can only judge by eye, so a green run meant
little while it was the only thing under test. The 2D plan of the store gave me
a flat, measurable stand-in, and once the checks held that, I started to trust
them. Changing the size of the room was no longer risky, since a wrong number
now showed up as a failing check straight away.

## What did this work change about who I want to be as a software developer?

I want to start from how things go in real life before I build.
When I was unsure what customer service needed, my instinct was to explain it
better to Claude. What helped was having it write out a whole visit from the
door to the till, because that showed me what I had left out. Choosing the
characters worked the same way. I could only tell which design was right by
seeing a few of them next to each other. Before this crit I would have kept
adding words to a prompt, and now I would rather put something real in front of
me first.
